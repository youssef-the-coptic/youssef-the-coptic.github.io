Who I am

A little about me

I'm a detail-oriented compliance and quality professional with more than eight years across quality assurance, regulatory and brand compliance, AI prompt development, digital marketing, and large-scale operations.

What ties it together is a habit of finding the repetitive work and replacing it with the right tool — so people spend their time on judgment instead of mechanics. Lately that's meant building automation: browser extensions, data pipelines, and AI-assisted review tools that my team uses every day.

I've led and trained teams of 400+, run multi-market operations across five languages, and earned my employer's Culture Ambassador award two years running. Away from the screen I'm a hiker, a chef, a retreat organiser, and someone who believes life is richer when you're fully present — whether scaling a peak or plating a meal for friends.

I'm also committed to giving back — from feeding the homeless to founding a blood-donation drive that has supported over 4,500 cases.

What I do now

Compliance, made faster

As a Partnership Marketing Compliance Specialist, I review affiliate and partner marketing for regulatory and brand compliance. Alongside the core role, I design and ship automation that streamlines how the whole team works — self-initiated, and adopted in daily use.

// 01

AI compliance scanning, end to end

Own the full build of an AI-powered scanning platform that reviews marketing assets against regulatory rules — from rule logic and glossary design through to a multi-month, multi-reviewer QA programme run with the external vendor and internal stakeholders.

// 02

A team-wide review tool

Designed and built a browser extension that consolidates a fragmented compliance review workflow into one interface — adopted across the team and in daily production use. It automates more than half of the manual actions per case.

// 03

Automation engines

Python pipelines that process and quality-check large compliance datasets each month — reverse-engineering the rules, validating at full recall, and turning hours of manual prep into a repeatable run.

// 04

Cross-team workflows

A bidirectional Slack ↔ Sheets automation that gave a partner-facing team end-to-end request tracking for the first time — built for a team outside my own, still owned and maintained by me.

// 05

AI-assisted QA

Using approved LLM tooling to audit standardised content against a canonical rulebook — surfacing gaps and anomalies that manual review would miss, and scoping the pipeline that comes next.

// 06

Reference tooling

An internal AI-assisted compliance knowledge hub, plus an interactive lookup tool covering regulatory status across 250 countries — turning scattered guidance into something the team can answer in seconds.
